Communication, Admin & Professional Growth — fast drafts, human-checked words

Three hands-on activities. First, build a privacy firewall: spot and redact the student details that must never reach a chatbot. Then tune the tone of a message home until it lands the way you'd want it to. Finally, assemble the entire week — plan, assess, feedback, communicate — into one workflow where a human signs off at every stage.

The rule that protects you and your students: anything you type into a public AI tool may be stored and used to train it. So the safe workflow is redact first, draft second, review always. AI can save you an hour of admin a day — but only if you never hand it a real name, and never send its words unread.

The tone-tuner

AI will happily generate a parent message in any register you ask for — which means you are the one who has to know what "right" sounds like. A message that's too cold, too vague, or too blunt can turn a solvable issue into a conflict. For each scenario, all four drafts are factually accurate. Pick the one whose tone would actually get you the outcome you want.

Capstone — assemble the week's workflow

This is where the whole week clicks together. A complete AI-assisted teaching cycle runs plan → assess → feedback → communicate, and at every stage a human reviews before anything is real. Tick each step as you commit to it. Every item carries a HUMAN CHECK tag — because that review is the workflow, not an add-on. Complete all four stages to earn your "you did it."

You did it.

You just assembled a complete AI-assisted teaching workflow — and at every single stage, a human stayed in charge. That's the whole philosophy of this course in one screen: AI drafts, the teacher decides.

  • ✓ Plan — AI drafts, you shape it to your real class and verify the facts.
  • ✓ Assess — rubric before marking, tasks designed so AI can't do the learning.
  • ✓ Feedback — AI's grade is a draft; the judgement and the tone are yours.
  • ✓ Communicate — privacy redacted first, every word read before it's sent.

Where this frees your time — and grows you

"Professional growth" isn't a buzzword here. The hours AI hands back on admin are hours you can spend on the parts of teaching only a human can do — and on getting better at your craft. Two honest framings to take with you.

+

Admin AI can genuinely lighten

Low-stakes, no-identity, high-repetition tasks — draft it fast, then skim.

  • First drafts of newsletters and notices (you add the specifics)
  • Reformatting your own notes into a lesson outline
  • Brainstorming differentiation ideas to choose from
  • Turning a bullet list into a polished, structured email
–

Where the human is the point

The relationship, the judgement, and the duty of care never delegate.

  • The final decision on any grade or report comment
  • A difficult conversation with a worried parent or child
  • Anything involving a real student's private data unredacted
  • Signing your name to words you haven't actually read
→

Growth mindset for the AI era: the teachers who thrive won't be the ones who memorise the trendiest tool — those change yearly. They'll be the ones fluent in the durable move you practised all week: let the machine draft, then bring your judgement, your knowledge of the child, and your professional standard to every word before it counts.
