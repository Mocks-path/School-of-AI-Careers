Bookkeeping, Reconciliation & Data — the accountant reviews, recomputes, and reconciles to source

AI can capture, extract, and categorise transactions in seconds — and get some of them quietly wrong. Three hands-on activities put you in the reviewer's seat: catch the errors in an AI-processed batch, find the discrepancy in a ledger-versus-bank reconciliation by recomputing to source, and order the bookkeeping workflow so that nothing skips the step that protects the record — reconciling every figure to source before it is trusted.

Progress
1

The reviewer's desk — catch the errors AI left behind

An AI tool has extracted and categorised a small batch of transactions from the source documents. It looks clean and confident. Your job is the accountant's job: review each entry against its source and decide whether to keep it or fix it. One is a mis-read amount, one is a wrong tax code, one is a hallucinated total, and one is genuinely correct. Read the source line under each entry, then choose.

Keep

The AI's extracted fields match the source document exactly — the amount, the code, and the total all tie out. Nothing to change.

Fix

Something does not tie to source — a wrong amount, an incorrect tax code, or a figure that appears nowhere in the data. It must be corrected before it is posted.

💡 The reviewer's instinct: AI extraction is a fast first pass, never the record. Every amount is checked against the source document, every code against the real rate, and every total is recomputed. A confident-looking entry that ties to nothing is exactly the one that misstates the accounts.

Reconcile to source — find the discrepancy yourself

An AI assistant summarised this reconciliation and declared the ledger and the bank "reconciled — balances agree." They do not. The ledger cash-book below lists four movements; the bank statement is the source of truth. Add up the ledger yourself, compare it to the bank's closing balance, and find the discrepancy. This is the habit that no tool replaces: recompute to source, never trust AI's arithmetic or its "reconciled" verdict.

Recompute the ledger from its own lines (12,000 + 3,450 − 1,200 − 50) and compare to the bank's 14,200.00 source closing. What is actually going on?
💡 The lesson: the ledger's own lines add to 14,200.00, but the "closing" AI printed was 14,150.00 — its arithmetic was wrong, and it still declared the accounts reconciled. Recomputing to source is what catches it. Never accept a "reconciled" verdict you have not reproduced yourself.
